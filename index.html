Why some rows are marked unreachable

The kangaroo method needs the target's public key — it walks between two known EC points. Puzzles whose pubkey was never revealed (the odd-numbered gaps, #71–#159) give you only a hash160. There is no pair of points to walk between, so the interval ECDLP attack is structurally impossible, not merely expensive — and there is no meet-in-the-middle shortcut either, because hash160 has no group structure. The only known attack is plain enumeration: 2^(n-1) expected hash160 evaluations. At 5e6 hash160/s per GPU that is ~7.5 million GPU-years for #71 — the smallest of them — and it grows by 2× with every puzzle number. That is why the census marks them "unreachable with known methods", and why a revealed pubkey (a single spend from the address) instantly turns one of these rows into a kangaroo-able prize. Watch for it: every so often a puzzle is "de-anonymized" by a spend, and the row jumps across the table.

Why fleet size changes your constant: the spacing/band geometry

The SOTA walk doesn't diffuse — it saturates in a band of width B ≈ 400·√W·(M/2^14)^0.4 around its start (paper §3.2). A herd of h kangaroos therefore tiles the interval with wild-start spacing 1.0625·W/(2h/3), and the governing geometric quantity is the ratio spacing/band (paper §5.5):

h ≳ 3·1.0625·W/(2·B)  keeps spacing/band ≲ 1. Under-tiling costs you +7–14% K (measured); over-tiling wastes hardware (−9…−22%, the DP formula over-penalizes). How to read the EV column

A kangaroo run's work-to-solution is exponentially distributed with mean K·√W. Committing exactly the mean budget succeeds with probability 1 − 1/e ≈ 63.2%, so solo EV = 0.632·prize − expected cost. Cost counts every GPU-hour your fleet burns: fleet size divides the wall time, never the total GPU-hours, so bigger fleets get there faster, not cheaper. A negative EV at your settings is not an insult — it is the honest answer to "can I afford this?", and the reason people pool. Pooling divides cost and prize by the same share, so EV per dollar is unchanged; what pooling buys is variance, not edge.
